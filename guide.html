What this is

A brand built in the open

People are moving their money toward smaller brands that see them. This line is getting ready for them — starting with the designs, and asking for opinions before anything gets made.

01

The mark

One bold T carries both

The footwear emblem is a silver shield that reads F.T.U. The single, oversized T stands for both T's in From Them To Us — fewer letters, so it stays crisp on a heel tab, a strap or the top of a board.

  • M W — men and women. The line is built for both.
  • Est. 2026 — the year it started.
  • From Them… To Us… — around the bottom edge, the whole idea in four words.

The footwear carries the shield. The clothing line will have its own F.T.T.U. lettering, like the one on the jacket.

The calendar

Four seasons, four looks

The guide follows meteorological seasons — whole calendar months — so the look changes cleanly on the 1st. The showroom stays the same all year.

Why whole months? The astronomical seasons start on the equinoxes and solstices, which land on a different day each year (in 2026: March 20, June 20, September 22 and December 21 in U.S. time zones). Switching on the 1st is simpler, and it's how weather services count seasons too. These dates are for the Northern Hemisphere.

The road ahead

Crawl, walk, run

Build the foundation first. Each phase only starts once the one before it works.

Phase 1 · Now

Show the line

The concept designs, this guide, and the showroom that lets anyone ask what exists and see it instantly.

Phase 2

Let people vote

Favorites and votes on each design, so the first styles to make are the ones people actually want.

Phase 3

Samples and partners

Turn the winning designs into real specs, find a manufacturer, and make test pairs.

Phase 4

Sizes and launch

Ladies' and men's versions, full size runs, and the clothing line behind the jacket.

Getting it made

The 5 W's of manufacturing

The questions the line has to answer to get from a render to a real shoe. Where there's no answer yet, it says so.

Who makes it?

Not chosen yet. Brands this size usually hire a contract manufacturer that builds to the brand's own designs.

  • Which factories make knit sneakers, boots and sandals?
  • Who backs early-stage founders, and who comes in later?
  • Who else is building in this space?

What gets made first?

Decided by the phase-two votes. Each winning design then needs technical specs a factory can build from.

  • Which one or two styles lead?
  • What materials and colors, exactly?
  • What does a first sample cost?

When does it happen?

No dates yet, on purpose. Votes come before any manufacturing conversation.

  • When to start talking to manufacturers?
  • When to approach investors?
  • When would a first run realistically ship?

Where does it come from?

Open. Location affects cost, quality checks and how fast samples arrive.

  • Where are the manufacturer and materials?
  • Where does the funding come from?
  • Where will it be sold — online first?

Why now?

Because people are actively redirecting what they spend. A brand that's ready when they look is in the right place.

  • Why a manufacturer instead of going solo?
  • Why partners, and which kind?
  • Why this line, and not later?

How you can help

  • Tell the showroom which designs you'd wear.
  • Say which season's colors you like best.
  • Tell us what's missing from the line.
