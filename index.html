What's real on this page

Live answers. The chat sends your conversation to a small backend (/api/chat) that calls the Claude API. Claude reasons only over the F.T.T.U catalog loaded on this page — 32 concept designs across 11 categories — and every reply is written fresh. Tap the big picture to see it full screen and swipe through the set. The three cards fill from that same catalog the moment a design is named, so nothing on the right is invented.

Concept stage, honestly. Every image is an AI-generated concept render. There are no sizes, no prices and nothing to buy yet. The showroom exists to show the line and hear what people think before anything is made.

What it can't do yet. It can't take a vote, save a favorite to an account, or place an order. Voting is planned for phase two and needs a small database behind it.

Voice stays on your device. The mic uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to turn speech into text before you send it. Chrome, Edge and Safari support it; where a browser doesn't, the mic hides itself.
